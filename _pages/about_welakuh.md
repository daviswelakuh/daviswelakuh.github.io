Davis' research is focused on light-matter interactions on a microscopic level in particular in the strong-coupling regime. He is interested in both the development of new computational methods to describe these regimes, e.g. quantum-electrodynamical density-functional theory (QEDFT), as well as their applications in catalysis, linear spectroscopy, nonlinear optics, quantum computing and quantum-information processing.

Davis joined New York University (NYU) in 2023 as a postdoctoral research fellow at the Simons Center for Computational Physical Chemistry. Prior to NYU, he was a postdoctoral research scholar at Harvard University at the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences. Davis earned his Ph.D. in theoretical physics from the Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter and the University of Hamburg, Germany.
